Final Year Project  ·  Computer Science

Vital Guardian

AI-Powered Real-Time ICU Patient Monitoring System
01
The Problem
Global nursing shortages make continuous bedside observation unscalable — delayed responses to critical patient events cost lives.
02
The Friction
Existing solutions rely on uncomfortable wearables or trigger excessive false alarms, causing dangerous alarm fatigue in clinical staff.
03
Our Objective
A continuous, non-invasive, multi-modal system with sub-50 ms critical event detection via AI sensor-fusion. No wearables. No raw data off-device.
System Architecture
Core Impact
🛡
Combating Alarm Fatigue
Multi-modal corroboration eliminates false-positive single-sensor triggers.
🤝
Staff Amplification
A tireless digital co-attendant scaling oversight without staffing increases.
🔒
Privacy by Design
Fully edge-deployed. Zero raw audio or video ever leaves the room.
🩺
Zero-Contact Monitoring
No wearables. Complete patient comfort preserved at all times.
